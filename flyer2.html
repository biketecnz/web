Biketec - Specialists in bikes for schools
We know how tough kids can be on bikes.
Our goal is to keep your school's fleet in top condition.
Join our School Bike Fleet Service Programme today!
Our mission is to provide a service that addresses exactly what a school needs to keep their full bike fleet ready and safe for their students to benefit from while limiting costs.

We see the results of how bikes are treated in schools every day. Over the past fourteen years we’ve seen it all, kids are tough on bikes. To keep your investment in equipment for your students in a safe, serviceable condition they need regular care. Bikes in a school environment that are not regularly maintained have a short lifespan, but with some care they can last well over ten years.

You could take the time to pile your bikes on a trailer and haul them down to a bike store and pay $80-$150 for each bike to get checked over and tightened up, plus more for replacement parts. Or you could join our School Bike Fleet Service Programme and know that your bikes will be ready to use year round.

An annual subscription includes:

  • We come to you, so you don’t have to transport bikes
  • We fully service every bike four times per year
  • We apply measures that reduce common breakages
  • No extra charge for most parts
  • Free call-outs between services for breakdowns
  • Activities for bike monitors to learn maintenance
  • Check bike storage/security and advise improvements
  • Share experience from other schools

Costs

Child sized bikes: $78 per bike per year (min 20)
Adult sized bikes: $112 per bike per year (min 20)
Scooters and balance bikes included free.
Prices exclude GST.

We also give advice on purchasing new bikes and equipment most appropriate for your environment and needs, and can supply a range of top-quality brands. All bikes we sell come with a one year subscription to this service included.
