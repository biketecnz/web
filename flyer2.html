Biketec - Specialists in bikes for schools
We know how tough kids can be on bikes. So our goal is to keep your school's fleet in top condition
Join our School Bike Fleet Service Programme today!
Our mission is to provide a service that addresses exactly what a school needs to keep their full bike fleet ready and safe for their students to benefit from while limiting costs.

We see the results of how bikes are treated in schools every day. Over the past fourteen years we’ve seen it all, kids are tough on bikes. To keep your investment in equipment for your students in a safe, serviceable condition they need regular care. Bikes in a school environment that are not regularly maintained have a short lifespan, but with some care they can last well over ten years.

You could take the time to pile your bikes on a trailer and haul them down to a bike store and pay $80-$150 for each bike to get checked over and tightened up, plus more for replacement parts, and that will keep them going for a while, or you could join our School Bike Fleet Service Programme and know that your bikes will be ready to use year round.

An annual subscription to the Biketec School Bike Fleet Service Programme includes

  • We come to you, so you don’t have to figure out how to get your bikes to a store on the other side of town,
  • We fully inspect, service, and tune every bike in your fleet four times per year,
  • We apply our own special measures that reduce common breakages,
  • We don’t charge extra for parts because we’re checking your bikes on a regular basis reducing wear and tear,
  • We drop by without charge to repair any out of action bikes between services,
  • We integrate activities during scheduled services for your bike monitors to get involved, teaching them to look after the bikes, recognise faults, and assist with maintenance,
  • We check your bike storage and security arrangements, and advise on improvements or maintenance required,
  • We share ideas we have gathered from our experience with schools across the region…
