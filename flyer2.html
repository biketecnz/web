Biketec - Specialists in bikes for schools

We know how tough kids can be on bikes.
Our goal is to keep your school's fleet in top condition.
Join our School Bike Fleet Service Programme today!
Our mission is to provide a service that addresses exactly what a school needs to keep their full bike fleet ready and safe for their students...

An annual subscription includes:

  • We come to you, no need to haul bikes to a store
  • We inspect, service, and tune every bike four times per year
  • We apply special measures to reduce common breakages
  • No extra charges for parts
  • We drop by free to repair out-of-action bikes

Cost

Child-sized bikes: $78 per bike/year
Adult-sized bikes: $112 per bike/year
(Min 20 bikes, excl. GST)
